SUBPROJECT 02

Global Youth Social Science Forum (The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen)

August 2026 | Participant & EU Delegate, Model United Nations

AI Governance Research
As an EU delegate, analyze policy approaches to AI regulation, technology company accountability and digital economy governance, with particular attention to taxation frameworks for major AI companies.
Coalition Building & Stakeholder Coordination
Coordinate with delegates from France, Germany, Italy and China to identify shared regulatory priorities and acceptable policy boundaries. Consult European and Asian delegations on technological innovation, corporate responsibility and fiscal oversight, addressing divergent positions and building consensus.
Draft Resolution Writing
Draft the final resolution as a member of the EU delegation, translating coalition positions and negotiated compromises into formal provisions and incorporating feedback from multiple delegations.

SUBPROJECT 03

“International Organizations and Global Justice” & “Financial Crises: Patterns and Lessons” (University of Cambridge, UK)

August 2025 | Presenter & Lead Researcher

International Organizations & Global Justice
Examine how international organizations shape global justice through rule-making, resource allocation and accountability, focusing on human rights, economic equity and climate justice, with FAO agricultural projects in South Sudan and Somalia as case studies.
Comparing International Financial Crises
Apply moral hazard, asset bubble and cross-border capital flow frameworks to compare the 1997 Thai financial crisis, the 2008 U.S. subprime mortgage crisis and China's real estate credit expansion.
Research Outputs
Lead a team in producing two research reports combining theoretical analysis and cross-national case studies, and present the findings.

SUBPROJECT 04

National Student Entrepreneurship Project: “Qingqing Ziqin, Shouyi Jiangxin”—Pioneering an E-Commerce Model Connecting Work-Study Programs with Rural Craft Heritage

September 2024–February 2025 | Operations Team Lead

Project Focus
Explore a model that connects work-study programs with rural craft preservation and e-commerce, supporting traditional craftsmanship and digital development.
Fieldwork & Practice
As stated in my CV, lead an interdisciplinary team in fieldwork in Ruyuan Yao Autonomous County, conducting over 200 interviews on intangible cultural heritage preservation and local economic development.
Digital Exploration & Outcomes
Apply digital marketing and e-commerce approaches to ethnic handicrafts to explore broader market access for traditional cultural products. The project received Silver in the Entrepreneurship category and Bronze in the Academic category of the GDUFS “Challenge Cup.”
