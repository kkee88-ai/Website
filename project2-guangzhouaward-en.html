PROJECT 02 · INTERNATIONAL COMMUNICATION · 2026

Global Communications for the Guangzhou International Award for Urban Innovation

International Communication Intern | Guangzhou Award Secretariat

01 · Context

Communicate urban governance innovations to domestic and international audiences for the 7th Guangzhou International Award for Urban Innovation, covering content planning, bilingual writing, international social media management and media coordination.

02 · My Role

  • • Manage a media network spanning Instagram, Facebook, X, WeChat and Weibo.
  • • Write bilingual press releases, social media copy and interview outlines for international guests.
  • • Coordinate with domestic and international media and multilateral partners to support conference publicity.

03 · Reflections

International communication is more than translation: it requires understanding audience contexts, selecting effective narratives and presenting real cases in ways that resonate across cultures.
