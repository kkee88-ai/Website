About Me

My Story

Explore Life Beyond Work

As the top-ranked student in my International Politics program, I have explored the intersection of policy research and international communication through practice rather than following a purely academic path.

My experience ranges from representing Malaysia in ASEAN digital economy negotiations at academic conferences and conducting over 200 interviews on intangible cultural heritage in rural northern Guangdong, to writing front-page coverage of Chinese companies going global. I then worked on global communications at the Guangzhou Award and now focus on AI policy research at NetEase Youdao.

Being a bridge builder connects my experiences: bridging academia and practice, China and the world, and policy and the public. Overseeing more than 100 student organizations strengthened my coordination skills, while cross-sector internships developed my capabilities across policy research, content production and cross-cultural communication. I work with both policy language and public-facing narratives, combining rigorous analysis with accessible communication.
