PROJECT 03 · FINANCIAL MEDIA · 2025

International Communication at Southern Finance Omnimedia Group

International Communication Intern | International Communication Department, Southern Finance Omnimedia Group

01 · Context

Work on financial reporting, interview planning, video content, KOL research and international student community management, focusing on Chinese corporate globalization and communication across ASEAN.

02 · My Role

  • • Co-author a front-page analytical report for the 21st Century Business Herald.
  • • Develop interview outlines for companies and industry experts and contribute to video series planning.
  • • Analyze audience profiles, content focus and engagement data for over 100 Southeast Asian TikTok and Instagram KOLs, developing a collaboration assessment framework.
  • • Found and manage an international student community of over 70 members and organize cross-cultural exchanges.

03 · Reflections

Financial communication requires evidence and data to support arguments. Cross-cultural communication requires an understanding of audience differences and a balance between professional depth and accessibility.
